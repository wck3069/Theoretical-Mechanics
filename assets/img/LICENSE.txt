Lanczos filter to 2048x1024 and re-encoded as progressive JPEG
(quality 88, 4:4:4 subsampling) on 2026-11-11 to keep the added payload
under 300 KB. Map projection (equirectangular) and image content are
unchanged; the renderer in neural/index.html does the spherical mapping.
